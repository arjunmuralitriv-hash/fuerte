## Which plan is right for me?

- **Bronze** if a short outage is an inconvenience, not a crisis.
- **Silver** if several systems depend on each other and downtime affects daily work.
- **Gold** if downtime affects safety, revenue, regulators or the public.

## What does every plan cover?

Networks and Wi-Fi, structured cabling, server rooms and data centers, servers and backup, CCTV, access control, fire alarm [CONFIRM: QCDD requirements], public address, BMS, audio-visual and unified communications. Each contract lists the exact assets covered.

## What's not included?

- Parts and consumables, unless your plan includes them
- Damage from misuse, accidents, power surges or unauthorised changes
- Faults that existed before the contract started (we quote these separately after the takeover audit)
- New installations, moves and additions (quoted separately)
