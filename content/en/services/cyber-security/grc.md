## What does PDPPL require from Qatari businesses?

PDPPL requires organisations to process personal data lawfully, transparently and only for clear purposes. You must respect individuals' rights, protect data with suitable controls, manage processors, and take care with marketing, sensitive data and children's data. Read our [PDPPL compliance checklist](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar).

## What is the NIA standard?

The National Information Assurance standard is Qatar's framework of information security controls, issued by the National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA). It applies to government entities and is widely used as a benchmark by critical sectors and their suppliers. [CONFIRM: current NIA version and applicability with NCSA guidance.]

## Frequently asked questions

### Who enforces PDPPL in Qatar?
The National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA) oversees data protection. [CONFIRM: current competent department name.]

### What are the penalties for breaching PDPPL?
Fines can reach up to QAR 5 million depending on the breach. [CONFIRM: penalty figures with counsel.]

### Do we need a data protection officer?
PDPPL does not use the same DPO rule as some other laws, but you need clear accountability for personal data. Many organisations appoint a privacy lead. [CONFIRM with counsel.]

### Can you help us get ISO 27001 certified?
We help you prepare: gap analysis, policies, risk assessment and internal audit. Certification itself is issued by an accredited certification body.

### Does PDPPL apply to CCTV footage?
Yes. Images of identifiable people are personal data. You need a clear purpose, signage, access controls and a retention period.
