# Vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT)

VAPT combines two checks. A vulnerability assessment scans your systems for known weaknesses. A penetration test has ethical hackers try to exploit them, the way a real attacker would. ## What is the difference between a vulnerability assessment and a penetration test?

A vulnerability assessment finds and lists possible weaknesses, mostly with automated tools. It is broad and fast. A penetration test goes further. A skilled tester tries to exploit weaknesses, chain them together and show what an attacker could actually reach. You need both: the scan for coverage, the test for real-world impact.

## How often should we run VAPT?

At least once a year, and after any major change such as a new application, network redesign or office move. Many regulators and clients expect annual testing. Internet-facing applications benefit from testing before every major release.

## Is penetration testing safe for live systems?

Yes, when planned properly. We agree scope, timing and limits in writing, avoid destructive tests on production, and keep a direct line to your team during testing. Higher-risk tests run out of hours or in a test environment.
