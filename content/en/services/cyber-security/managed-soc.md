## What does a SOC actually do?

A SOC watches for attacks around the clock. Analysts review alerts from your systems, separate real threats from false alarms, and act when something is wrong. It is the difference between finding a breach in minutes and finding it months later.

## Why outsource your SOC instead of building one?

A 24/7 in-house SOC needs several shifts of trained analysts, expensive tools and constant tuning. For most organisations, a managed SOC delivers the same coverage faster and for a predictable monthly fee. You keep control of decisions; we do the watching.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between SOC, MDR and SIEM?
A SIEM is the tool that collects and analyses logs. A SOC is the team and process that watches it. Managed detection and response (MDR) is a SOC service that also acts on threats, not only alerts on them.

### Do we need to buy new tools?
Not always. We can use your existing firewall, EDR and log sources where they are suitable, and fill gaps where needed.
