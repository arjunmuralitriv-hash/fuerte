# Security awareness training

Security awareness training teaches your staff to spot and stop cyber attacks, especially phishing, fake invoices and social engineering. Fuerte runs tailored training programmes in Qatar, in Arabic and English, combining short sessions, realistic phishing simulations and clear reporting. The result is a workforce that recognises threats and reports them quickly, which reduces the human risk behind most breaches.

## What's included

- Baseline phishing simulation to measure starting risk
- Live or online sessions, in Arabic and English
- Role-based modules for executives, finance, HR, IT and front-line staff
- Regular phishing simulations with instant teachable moments
- Topics: phishing, passwords and MFA, social engineering, safe remote work, data protection and PDPPL basics, physical security
- Short quizzes and completion certificates
- Reports for management showing progress over time
- Annual refresher programme

## Why is training needed if we have security tools?

Tools block most attacks, but some always reach the inbox. A well-crafted phishing email or a convincing phone call only needs one person to click or share a password. Trained staff are your last line of defence and often your fastest detection.

## How do phishing simulations work?

We send safe, realistic test emails to staff. If someone clicks, they see a short lesson on what they missed. Nobody is named or shamed in management reports unless you choose otherwise. Results show where to focus training.

## How often should staff be trained?

Run a full programme at least once a year, with short monthly or quarterly simulations. Train new joiners in their first weeks.
