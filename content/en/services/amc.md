# AMC and support

An annual maintenance contract (AMC) from Fuerte keeps your IT and ELV systems working all year. It combines scheduled preventive maintenance, a helpdesk, agreed response times for faults and clear reports. We cover networks, data centers, CCTV, access control, fire alarm, PA, BMS and audio-visual systems, including systems another contractor installed.

## What's included

- Scheduled preventive maintenance visits with a checklist for every device type
- A helpdesk by phone, email and WhatsApp
- Corrective maintenance with agreed response and restoration times
- Firmware and software updates, tested before rollout
- Health checks on cameras, storage, UPS, cooling, network and fire panels
- An asset register that stays up to date
- Maintenance reports after every visit and a quarterly service review
- Optional spare-parts holding and 24/7 cover

## How do the Bronze, Silver and Gold plans differ?

Each plan sets the response time, the number of preventive visits and the hours of cover. [Compare AMC plans](/amc-plans)

## Can you take over maintenance of systems someone else installed?

Yes. We start with a takeover audit. We check every device, record its condition, fix urgent faults and agree what is covered. Faults that existed before the contract started are quoted separately.
