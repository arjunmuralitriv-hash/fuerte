# ELV systems

ELV (extra-low voltage) systems are the low-power building systems that keep people safe and buildings running: CCTV, access control, fire alarm, public address, building management, audio-visual and automation. As an ELV contractor, Fuerte designs, installs, integrates and maintains all of them across Qatar, so your systems share one network, one design and one support contract.

## Why integrate ELV systems instead of buying them separately?

- **One network.** Cameras, access readers and PA speakers share a properly designed, secured network.
- **Systems that talk.** A fire alarm can unlock doors, call up cameras and trigger voice evacuation automatically.
- **One control room.** Operators see everything on one screen in a [command and control center](/services/cctv).
- **Lower lifetime cost.** One maintenance contract and one spare-parts strategy.

## Do your ELV systems meet Qatari approval requirements?

Many ELV systems in Qatar need approval before handover. CCTV is regulated by the Ministry of Interior Security Systems Department (MoI SSD). Fire alarm systems fall under the Qatar Civil Defence Department (QCDD). We design to these requirements and prepare the submissions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does ELV stand for?
Extra-low voltage. It covers building systems that run at low voltage, typically under 50 V AC, such as CCTV, access control, fire alarm, public address and data networks.

### Can you integrate with systems we already have?
Usually, yes. We check protocols and licences first, then integrate through standard interfaces such as ONVIF, BACnet, Modbus or vendor APIs.

### Do you handle the cabling as well?
Yes. ELV systems depend on [structured cabling](/services/structured-cabling), and we design both together.

### Who maintains the systems after handover?
We do, under an [AMC](/services/amc) with preventive visits and agreed response times.
