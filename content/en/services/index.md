## How we deliver every project

1. **Site survey.** We visit, measure and listen. Free of charge.
2. **Design and proposal.** Drawings, bill of quantities, schedule and a fixed quote.
3. **Approvals.** We prepare the submissions your project needs, such as consultant, MoI SSD or Civil Defence review where applicable.
4. **Installation.** Our own engineers, a named project manager and a written HSE plan.
5. **Testing and handover.** Test certificates, as-built drawings, configuration backups and user training.
6. **Support.** An AMC with agreed response times, so the system keeps working.
