## Why does a data center need one accountable team?

A server room is a system. Power, cooling, cabling, fire suppression and IT all affect each other. When different contractors deliver each part, gaps appear: cooling sized for yesterday's racks, UPS without monitoring, cabling that blocks airflow. We design the whole room together, then monitor it as one.

## What does a typical project look like?

1. **Assess.** Measure current load, power, cooling and space. Agree availability targets.
2. **Design.** Layout, containment, power and cooling calculations, fire suppression and cabling.
3. **Build.** Civil and electrical coordination, installation, cable management and labelling.
4. **Commission.** Load-bank and failover tests, monitoring set-up, documentation.
5. **Operate.** Preventive maintenance and 24/7 environmental alerts under an [AMC](/services/amc).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you upgrade a live server room without downtime?
In most cases, yes. We phase the work, use temporary power and cooling where needed and schedule cut-overs out of hours, with a tested rollback plan.

### Do you design to a tier standard?
We design to the availability level you need and can align to recognised frameworks such as Uptime Institute tiers or TIA-942. [CONFIRM: any formal data center design certifications held by Fuerte staff.]

### What is a modular data center?
A pre-engineered unit (rack, row or container) that includes racks, cooling, power and monitoring. It is faster to deploy than a traditional build and easy to expand.

### Do you provide monitoring after handover?
Yes. We install DC monitoring software for temperature, humidity, power, leaks and door access, and can watch the alerts for you under an AMC.
