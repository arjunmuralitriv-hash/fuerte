# Data center network

A data center network connects servers, storage and the outside world at high speed with no single point of failure. Fuerte designs, installs, operates and supports data center networks in Qatar, including spine-leaf fabrics, core switching, high-density fibre, segmentation and monitoring. We deliver both the active switches and the passive cabling, so performance is proven end to end.

## What's included

- Traffic analysis and capacity planning
- Spine-leaf or collapsed-core architecture design
- 10, 25, 40 and 100 Gbps switching
- High-density fibre trunks, MPO/MTP cabling and patching
- Redundant uplinks, link aggregation and failover testing
- Micro-segmentation and firewalling between zones
- Connection to storage networks and the campus network
- Monitoring, configuration backup and documentation
- Ongoing operations and [AMC](/services/amc) support

## What is a spine-leaf network?

A two-layer design. Every leaf switch, at the top of a rack, connects to every spine switch. Any server can reach any other in the same number of hops, which keeps latency low and predictable. To grow, you add leaves or spines without redesigning the network.

## Why does segmentation matter in a data center?

If an attacker reaches one server, segmentation stops them moving freely to others. We separate production, management, backup and building systems, and control traffic between them. It is one of the most effective defences against ransomware.

## How do you migrate a live data center network?

We build the new fabric alongside the old one, test it, then move servers in planned waves during agreed windows. Every wave has a rollback plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do we need 100 Gbps?
Not always. We size the network from your real traffic and growth, then choose speeds that give headroom without overspending.

### Can you reuse our existing fibre?
Often, yes. We test it first. Older multi-mode fibre may limit distances at higher speeds.

### Do you support software-defined networking?
Yes. We can design fabrics with automation and centralised policy where the scale justifies it.

### Who supports the network after go-live?
We do, under an AMC with monitoring and agreed response times.
