## Server room or modular data center: what's the difference?

A traditional server room is built into an existing space. It suits most offices and campuses. A modular data center is a pre-engineered rack, row or container with its own cooling, power and monitoring. It is faster to deploy, easy to relocate and grows in steps. We help you choose based on load, space, budget and timeline.

## How do you cool a server room in Qatar's climate?

Comfort air conditioning is not enough. We use precision cooling sized for your IT load, with N+1 redundancy where needed. Containment stops hot and cold air from mixing, which lowers energy use and hot spots. We also plan for high outdoor temperatures when sizing condensers.

## What is FM-200 and why is it used?

FM-200 is a clean-agent gas that puts out fires quickly without water and without damaging electronics. It leaves no residue, so equipment can keep running after a discharge. It is common in server rooms and electrical rooms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a server room build take?
A small room usually takes 6 to 12 weeks from design approval. Larger data centers take longer, depending on civil and electrical works and equipment lead times.

### Can you build in an existing room?
Yes. We survey the space, floor loading, power supply and cooling options first, then design around them.

### Do you coordinate with our main contractor and MEP consultant?
Yes. We work within the project's approval process and coordinate drawings with the MEP and civil teams.

### What availability can you design for?
We design to the availability level your business needs, from a single UPS and cooling unit to fully redundant N+1 or 2N designs, and can align with TIA-942 or Uptime Institute tier concepts.

### Do you monitor the room after handover?
Yes. Our DC monitoring sends alerts by email or SMS, and we can respond under an [AMC](/services/amc).
