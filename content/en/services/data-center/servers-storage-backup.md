## What is the 3-2-1 backup rule?

Keep 3 copies of your data, on 2 different types of storage, with 1 copy off site. Many organisations now add one immutable or offline copy that ransomware cannot change. We design every backup to this rule.

## What are RPO and RTO?

The recovery point objective (RPO) is how much data you can afford to lose, measured in time. The recovery time objective (RTO) is how long you can afford to be down. We agree both with you for each system, then design backup and DR to meet them.

## How often should backups be tested?

At least quarterly for critical systems, and after every major change. A backup you have never restored is a hope, not a plan. Our AMC plans include scheduled restore tests and written results.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can backups protect us from ransomware?
Yes, if they are designed for it. Immutable or offline copies cannot be encrypted by attackers. We pair them with [managed security monitoring](/services/managed-soc) to spot attacks early.

### Should our backups be in the cloud?
Cloud is a good off-site copy. If your data must stay in Qatar, we choose a local or in-country cloud region. We check your [PDPPL](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar) and sector rules first.

### Do you support existing servers from other vendors?

### How quickly can we recover a server?
That depends on the RTO we design for. Critical systems can be designed to restart in minutes from replicas; others can be restored in hours from backup.
