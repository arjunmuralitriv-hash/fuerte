# Technology services

Fuerte's technology services cover network design and operations, structured cabling, unified communications, business applications and IT consulting. We design, install, run and support both active equipment (switches, firewalls, access points) and passive infrastructure (cabling, racks, patching) for organisations across Qatar, so your IT foundation comes from one accountable team.

## Why choose one partner for network, cabling and IT?

- **No finger-pointing.** If the link is down, one team owns the fix, from the patch panel to the firewall policy.
- **Right first time.** Cabling is designed for the network that will run on it, including PoE loads and future speeds.
- **Security from day one.** Firewalls, segmentation and hardened configurations come as standard, backed by our [cyber security](/services/cyber-security) team.
- **Supported for years.** Every installation can move straight into an [AMC](/services/amc).

## How do we deliver technology projects?

1. Survey the site and review your current network and applications.
2. Design the architecture, cable routes and equipment list.
3. Submit a fixed proposal with a bill of quantities and schedule.
4. Install, configure and test, with out-of-hours cut-overs for live sites.
5. Hand over certificates, as-built drawings and configuration backups.
6. Support under an agreed SLA.
