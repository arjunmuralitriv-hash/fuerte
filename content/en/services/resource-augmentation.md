# Resource augmentation

Fuerte's resource augmentation (IT staff augmentation) service places trained, certified and experienced IT and ELV engineers inside your team in Qatar. You choose fixed-cost or time-and-materials terms. Use it to cover a project peak, a skills gap, a long leave period or a new system go-live, without the delay and overhead of permanent hiring.

## Fixed-cost or time and materials: which model fits?

| | Fixed-cost | Time and materials |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | A defined scope with a clear end | Ongoing or changing work |
| You pay | An agreed price for agreed deliverables | An agreed rate for hours or days worked |
| Flexibility | Changes go through a change request | Scale up or down with notice |
| Risk on overruns | Mostly with Fuerte | Shared, managed through reporting |
