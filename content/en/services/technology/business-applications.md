## How long does an ERP implementation take?

A focused finance and inventory roll-out for a mid-sized business often takes 3 to 6 months. Multi-entity or highly customised projects take longer. We give you a phased plan during the proposal so you know what goes live when.

## Cloud or on-premises: which is right?

Cloud applications are faster to start and cheaper up front. On-premises systems give more control over where data sits. Some government and regulated clients need data held in Qatar. We review your data rules, including [PDPPL](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar) obligations, before recommending a model.

## Why do ERP projects fail, and how do you avoid it?

Most fail because of unclear requirements, poor data and weak training, not the software. We fix the process first, clean the data before migration and train users with real examples from your business.
