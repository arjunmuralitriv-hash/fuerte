# IT consulting and roadmaps

Fuerte's IT consulting team helps organisations in Qatar decide what to build, in what order and at what cost. We assess your current infrastructure, design the target architecture and deliver a costed, multi-year technology roadmap. We use a design, develop, deploy method, so every recommendation can be delivered in practice, by us or by your team.

## What's included

- Infrastructure assessment of network, data center, end-user, security and ELV
- Interviews with leadership and IT staff to capture goals and pain points
- Risk and gap analysis against good practice and your regulatory needs
- Target architecture and high-level design
- A 1-to-3-year roadmap with phases, budgets and quick wins
- Tender documents, specifications and BOQ preparation for your procurement
- Independent technical review of third-party proposals
- Board-ready summary presentation

## When should you bring in an IT consultant?

Common triggers include a new building or office move, a merger, an audit finding, a major outage, an end-of-life platform, or a budget cycle that needs a credible plan. A short assessment early saves expensive rework later.

## What do you get at the end?

A written report and roadmap that tells you where you stand today, where you need to be, and the exact steps, costs and timeline to get there. Each recommendation is ranked by business impact and effort.

## Is your advice independent?

Yes. We are vendor-neutral and recommend what fits your needs. If you prefer, we can scope the consulting so that we are excluded from bidding on the delivery work.
