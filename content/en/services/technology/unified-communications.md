# Unified communications

Unified communications brings calls, voicemail, video and messaging onto one platform. Fuerte designs, installs and supports VoIP and IP-PBX phone systems for businesses in Qatar. That includes desk phones, softphones and mobile apps, connections to your telecom provider, call recording, and contact center features, all running securely on your existing network.

## What's included

- Assessment of your current phone system, users and call flows
- On-premises, hybrid or hosted IP-PBX design
- IP desk phones, conference phones, softphones and mobile apps
- Connection to your telecom provider through SIP trunks or existing lines [CONFIRM: carriers Fuerte integrates with]
- Auto-attendant (IVR) in Arabic and English
- Call recording, call queues, reporting and contact center features
- Voice VLANs, quality of service (QoS) and PoE planning
- Integration with Microsoft Teams, CRM or door intercoms where needed
- Number porting coordination, user training and admin handover

## Why move from an old PBX to VoIP?

VoIP lowers line costs, lets staff take office calls anywhere and makes adding users simple. It also gives you call reporting and recording that legacy systems cannot. Most analogue and ISDN systems are also running out of spare parts.

## Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

In most cases, yes. We coordinate number porting or re-routing with your telecom provider so your published numbers keep working. [CONFIRM: process with local carriers.]

## How do you keep voice calls clear?

We put voice traffic on its own VLAN and give it priority with quality of service (QoS) settings. We also check bandwidth, PoE budgets and Wi-Fi roaming before go-live, then test call quality on every floor.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is VoIP legal in Qatar?
Business IP telephony through licensed telecom providers is common in Qatar. Some internet calling apps are restricted. We design systems that connect through a licensed carrier. [CONFIRM: wording with Qatari counsel or CRA guidance.]

### Can staff use the phone system on their mobile?
Yes. A mobile app turns a smartphone into an office extension, with the company number shown to callers.

### Do you support Microsoft Teams calling?
Yes. We can connect your phone system to Teams so staff call from one app.

### What happens if the internet goes down?
We design for resilience, with backup lines or failover routes for critical numbers.

### How many users can an IP-PBX support?
From a few users to several thousand, depending on the platform. We size it for your growth.
