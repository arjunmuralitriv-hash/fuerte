# Network design and operations

Fuerte handles enterprise network design, installation and operations for organisations in Qatar. We cover switching, routing, next-generation firewalls (NGFW), enterprise Wi-Fi planned with heat-mapping, and network monitoring (NMS). Every network is segmented, hardened and documented before handover, then supported under an agreed service level so it stays fast and secure.

## What's included

- Requirements workshop and survey of your current network
- High-level and low-level design (HLD and LLD), including IP plan and VLAN segmentation
- Core, distribution and access switching, with PoE budgets for cameras, phones and access points
- Routing, SD-WAN and internet edge design
- Next-generation firewall (NGFW) policies, VPN and remote access
- Predictive and on-site Wi-Fi surveys with heat-maps, before and after installation
- Network monitoring (NMS) with alerts, dashboards and capacity reports
- Configuration backups, as-built diagrams and an operations handbook
- Optional managed operations and [AMC](/services/amc)

## What makes a business network reliable?

Four things: redundancy, segmentation, monitoring and documentation. We build two paths where an outage would hurt, separate users, guests, cameras and building systems into their own segments, monitor every critical link and keep accurate records, so faults are found in minutes, not days.

## How does Wi-Fi heat-mapping work?

We model your floor plan, walls and expected users in planning software to place access points. After installation, we walk the site with survey tools and measure real signal strength, interference and roaming. You get a before-and-after heat-map that proves coverage in every room.

## Can you upgrade our network without downtime?

Yes, for most sites. We stage and pre-configure equipment off site, migrate one segment at a time and schedule cut-overs out of hours. Each change has a tested rollback plan.

### Do you configure firewalls as well as switches?

### What is a network monitoring system (NMS)?
Software that watches your switches, routers, firewalls and links, and alerts us when something fails or slows down. It also shows trends, so we can plan upgrades before capacity runs out.

### How many access points do we need?
It depends on building materials, layout and user density. A Wi-Fi survey gives you the exact number and positions. As a rough guide, open offices need fewer than classrooms or halls.
