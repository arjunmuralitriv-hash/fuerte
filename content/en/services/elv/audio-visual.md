# Audio-visual and smart meeting rooms

Fuerte designs, installs and supports audio-visual (AV) solutions and smart meeting rooms in Qatar, from small huddle rooms to boardrooms and auditoriums. We deliver interactive panels, displays, video conferencing, microphones, speakers, room control and booking. The aim is a room where any meeting starts with one touch and everyone can see and hear clearly.

## What's included

- Room survey and AV design, including sightlines and acoustics
- Interactive flat panels and digital whiteboards
- Displays, projectors and LED video walls
- Video conferencing for Microsoft Teams, Zoom and other platforms
- Ceiling and table microphones, speakers and DSP audio
- Wireless presentation and screen sharing
- Room control panels and room booking screens
- Simultaneous interpretation support for Arabic and English events [CONFIRM: scope]
- Digital signage
- Cabling, mounting, testing, training and an [AMC](/services/amc)

## What makes a meeting room "smart"?

Everything works from one control: the screen wakes, the call starts, the camera frames the speaker and the room shows as booked. Users don't need to find cables or remotes. The IT team can monitor and update rooms remotely.

## How do you make remote participants hear clearly?

Good audio matters more than video. We choose microphones for the room size, add echo cancellation, and treat hard surfaces where needed. Then we test from the far end before handover.

## Which video conferencing platform do you support?

We design rooms certified for the platform you use, most often Microsoft Teams Rooms or Zoom Rooms, and can support more than one where needed.
