# Home and building automation

Fuerte designs, installs and supports home and building automation in Qatar. We connect lighting, air conditioning, blinds, audio, intercom, CCTV and access into one system, controlled from wall panels, a mobile app or voice. It suits villas, apartments, offices and compounds, saves energy in Qatar's climate and makes daily routines effortless.

## KNX or wireless: which automation system is best?

KNX is a wired, open standard. It is very reliable and best planned during construction or major renovation. Wireless systems are quicker to retrofit in finished homes. Many projects use a mix. We recommend based on the build stage, budget and how much you want to control.

## Can home automation save energy in Qatar?

Yes. Cooling is usually the largest energy cost in a Qatari home. Scheduling, occupancy sensing and automatic blinds that block afternoon sun reduce wasted cooling without sacrificing comfort.

## Is a smart home secure?

It is when designed properly. We put automation devices on their own network segment, change default passwords, keep firmware updated and use encrypted remote access.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you automate a home that is already built?
Yes. Wireless and hybrid systems let us automate finished homes with minimal disruption.

### Can we control everything from one app?
Yes. We integrate systems so you use one app instead of several.

### What happens if the internet goes down?
Local control keeps working. Lights, climate and scenes run without the internet; only remote access pauses.

### Can the system grow later?
Yes. We design with spare capacity so you can add rooms and features over time.
