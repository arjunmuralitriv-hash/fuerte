# Public address systems

Fuerte designs, installs and maintains public address (PA) systems in Qatar, for small offices up to large campuses, malls and hotels. We deliver paging, background music and voice evacuation, with zoning so each area hears the right message. Our PA systems connect to the fire alarm so evacuation messages play automatically and clearly.

## What's included

- Acoustic survey and speaker layout design
- Zoning plan for paging, music and evacuation
- Ceiling, wall, horn and outdoor speakers
- Amplifiers, mixers and IP-based audio systems
- Paging microphones and scheduled announcements, such as bells or prayer-time messages
- Voice evacuation with pre-recorded Arabic and English messages
- Fire alarm interface and supervised speaker lines
- Intelligibility testing at handover
- Training and preventive maintenance under an [AMC](/services/amc)

## What is a voice evacuation system?

A PA system designed for emergencies. It plays clear, pre-recorded instructions when the fire alarm activates, and can run phased evacuation zone by zone. Spoken instructions tell people exactly what to do and where to go, which a bell or siren cannot. It must be supervised, so faults on speaker lines are reported.

## How do you make announcements clear in large spaces?

Clarity depends on speaker type, spacing and room acoustics, not just volume. We model the space, choose the right speakers and measure speech intelligibility on site before handover.

## Can we use our PA system over the network?

Yes. IP-based PA systems run over your data network, which makes it easy to add zones, schedule messages and page from a PC or phone. We design the network with the right priority for audio.
