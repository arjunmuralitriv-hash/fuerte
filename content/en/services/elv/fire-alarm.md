## Addressable or conventional fire alarm: what's the difference?

A conventional system groups detectors into zones and tells you which zone is in alarm. An addressable system identifies the exact device. Addressable systems find fires faster, reduce false alarms and are standard for medium and large buildings. Conventional systems can suit small sites.

## Do fire alarm systems in Qatar need Civil Defence approval?

Yes. In Qatar, fire alarm design, products and installation are subject to QCDD approval, and buildings need a Civil Defence certificate. We prepare drawings and documents for submission and support inspections. [CONFIRM: exact submission route and whether Fuerte or a registered partner submits.]

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you integrate the fire alarm with other systems?
Yes. We link it to access control, PA voice evacuation, lift recall, HVAC shutdown and BMS through a tested cause-and-effect matrix.

### How do you reduce false alarms?
We choose the right detector for each space, use multi-sensor detectors where needed, position devices correctly and maintain them.

### Can you take over maintenance of an existing system?
Yes. We start with a full inspection and report, then maintain it under an AMC.
