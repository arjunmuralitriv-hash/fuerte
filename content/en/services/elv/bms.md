# Building management systems (BMS)

A building management system (BMS) monitors and controls a building's mechanical and electrical plant from one place. As a BMS contractor in Qatar, Fuerte designs, installs, integrates and maintains these systems, covering HVAC, chillers, lighting, pumps, energy meters and alarms. With Qatar's cooling loads, a well-tuned BMS cuts energy use, extends equipment life and spots faults early.

## What's included

- Survey of plant, existing controls and points list
- BMS architecture and controller design
- Direct digital controllers (DDC), sensors, actuators and valves
- Integration with chillers, AHUs, FCUs, VRF, lighting, lifts, generators and meters
- Open protocols: BACnet, Modbus, KNX and LonWorks
- Graphics, dashboards, alarms, trends and reports
- Energy monitoring and optimisation strategies
- Integration with fire alarm, access control and CCTV
- Cyber-secured BMS network and remote access
- Testing, commissioning, training and an [AMC](/services/amc)

## How does a BMS save energy?

It runs equipment only when needed and at the right level. Schedules, occupancy sensing, optimised chiller sequencing and temperature set-backs reduce wasted cooling, which is often the largest energy cost in a Qatari building. Trend data then shows where more savings are possible.

## Can a BMS work with equipment from different brands?

Yes, if the equipment supports open protocols such as BACnet or Modbus. We check every interface during design and use gateways where needed.

## Is a BMS a cyber security risk?

It can be. BMS controllers are often connected to the network with weak default settings. We segment the BMS network, secure remote access and change default credentials, guided by our [cyber security](/services/cyber-security) team.
