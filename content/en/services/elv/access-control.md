# Access control and intrusion detection

Fuerte designs, installs and maintains access control and intrusion detection systems in Qatar. We control who enters which door, gate or lift, and when, using cards, mobile credentials or biometrics. We also install intrusion alarms that detect break-ins. Both systems integrate with CCTV and fire alarm, so every event is seen and recorded.

- Turnstiles, speed gates, barriers and vehicle gates

- Intrusion detection: motion sensors, glass-break, door contacts, panic buttons and sirens

- Integration with CCTV, fire alarm and time attendance
- Fail-safe and fail-secure door design aligned with fire safety
- Testing, handover, admin training and an [AMC](/services/amc)

## Card, mobile or biometric: which credential is best?

| Credential | Strengths | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Card or fob | Low cost, familiar | Can be lent or lost |
| Mobile | Easy to issue and revoke remotely | Needs phone battery and app adoption |
| Biometric | Cannot be shared | Personal data under PDPPL; needs clear consent and policy |

Many sites combine them: a card for most doors and biometrics for high-security rooms.

## How does access control work with the fire alarm?

When the fire alarm activates, doors on escape routes must release so people can get out. We wire and program this link and test it with your fire alarm contractor or ours, so security never blocks an evacuation.

## Is biometric data covered by data protection law?

Yes. Fingerprints and face templates are personal data under Qatar's [PDPPL](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar). We help you set a clear purpose, notice and retention period, and we configure systems to store templates securely.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can access control record attendance?
Yes. Access events can feed a time-attendance or HR system.

### Can we lock down the whole site with one button?
Yes. We can configure lockdown modes for emergencies, tested with your security team.

### Can you add doors to our existing system?
Usually. We check the controller platform and licences first.

### Can we manage access from the cloud?
Yes. Cloud-managed systems are available. If you need on-premises control or local data storage, we design for that.

### What happens during a power cut?
Controllers and locks run on backup batteries. We size them for your required backup time and set each door to fail safe or fail secure.
