# About Fuerte

Fuerte Technologies & Contracting Services is a Doha-based technology and contracting company. We design, install, secure and maintain the systems modern buildings run on: networks, data centers, ELV systems and cyber security.

Most clients come to us because they are tired of managing five contractors for one building. We give them one partner, one plan and one number to call.

## Who we are

We are engineers, project managers and security specialists working from our office in Umm Ghuwailina, Doha. We combine technology services with contracting delivery. That means we can advise on the right design and then build it on site, with our own teams, to a standard we are willing to support for years.

## Our vision

To be the partner Qatar's organisations trust to run their technology without interruption.

Technology changes fast. Buildings and businesses need systems that keep up without constant rework. We stay current with the tools, methods and standards that matter, so our clients get solutions that hold up as their needs grow.

We see a future where businesses do more than keep the lights on. They grow, because the integration of technology and contracting is handled well and stays out of their way.

Our vision is also about people. Real success comes from understanding each client's goals and building partnerships that last beyond a single project.

## Our mission

To help businesses grow by combining reliable technology with disciplined contracting.

- **Lead with the client.** Every design starts from your goals, your site and your budget.
- **Keep contracting simple.** Clear scopes, clear schedules, clear handover.
- **Stay agile.** Respond quickly when your plans or your environment change.
- **Act responsibly.** Ethical practice, investment in skills and a positive contribution to the community.

## The zero-downtime promise

Downtime costs money, trust and sometimes safety. Our approach is built to prevent it.

- **Design for resilience.** Redundant paths, correct power and cooling, and spare capacity where it counts.
- **Install without disruption.** Phased cut-overs, out-of-hours work and tested rollback plans for live sites.
- **Hand over properly.** As-built drawings, test certificates, configuration backups and trained users.
- **Maintain proactively.** Preventive maintenance and monitoring catch problems before your users do.
- **Respond fast.** Clear response times under our [AMC plans](/amc-plans).

"Zero downtime" is the standard we design and work to. Actual availability depends on each system's design, budget and the service level agreed in your contract.

## Health, safety and environment (HSE)

Safe sites are well-run sites. Every project follows a written HSE plan.

- Site-specific risk assessments and method statements before work starts
- Toolbox talks and permit-to-work procedures on active sites
- Trained and equipped staff, including working at height and electrical safety
- Tidy sites, correct waste disposal and recycling of packaging and cable offcuts
- Incident reporting and lessons learned shared across the team
