## Request the pack

We send the pack by email, so we know who has our company documents.

**Form**

- **Full name** (required)
- **Job title** (required)
- **Organisation** (required)
- **Work email** (required). Helper: "We'll send the download link here."
- **Phone** (optional). Prefix: +974
- **What is this for?** (optional) Options: Supplier registration · Tender or RFP · Prequalification · Other
- **Tender or reference number** (optional)
- Consent (required, unticked): "I agree that Fuerte may use these details to send the vendor pack and follow up on this request, as described in the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy)."
- Marketing consent (optional, unticked): "Send me occasional updates about Fuerte's services. I can unsubscribe at any time."
- **Button:** Email me the vendor pack

**Success message:** "Check your inbox. We've sent the vendor pack to {email}. The link works for 7 days. [CONFIRM: link expiry] Can't see it? Check your spam folder or call +974 7125 8588."

**Error message (send failed):** "We couldn't send the pack just now. Please try again, or email contact@fuertetechnologies.com and we'll send it directly."
