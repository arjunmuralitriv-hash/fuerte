# AI assistant notice

**Last updated:** [PLACEHOLDER: date]

Our website, WhatsApp and phone lines may offer an AI assistant. This notice explains what it is and how it uses your information.

## You are talking to an AI

The assistant is an automated system, not a person. It uses a large language model provided by [CONFIRM: AI provider, e.g. Anthropic] to understand your questions and reply in Arabic or English. Every conversation starts with a clear message saying you are talking to an AI.

## What it can do

- Answer questions about our services, using only Fuerte's published information
- Help you describe your project and request a quote
- Book a site survey
- Log a support ticket for AMC clients
- Connect you to a member of our team

## What it cannot do

- It cannot give legal, regulatory, financial or safety advice.
- It cannot give binding prices. Any figure is an estimate until we send a written quote.
- It cannot make commitments on Fuerte's behalf.
- It can make mistakes. Check important information with our team.

## Talk to a person at any time

Type "human" or "agent", or press **Talk to a person**. During office hours we will connect you. Outside office hours, we'll create a request and call you back. You can also call +974 7125 8588.

## What happens to your messages

- **What we collect:** your messages, any contact details you share, and technical data such as time and language.
- **Why:** to answer you, handle your request, keep a record of what was agreed and improve the service.
- **Who sees it:** authorised Fuerte staff and our AI and hosting providers, under written agreements. [CONFIRM: provider list]
- **Training:** our AI provider does not use your conversations to train its models. [CONFIRM: provider terms]
- **Storage location:** [CONFIRM: region]
- **Retention:** conversation logs are kept for [CONFIRM: e.g. 12 months] for quality, security and audit, then deleted. Requests that become leads or tickets follow our [privacy notice](/legal/privacy).

Please don't share passwords, payment card details, ID numbers or health information with the assistant.

## Voice calls

If you speak to our voice assistant, the call is processed by speech technology so the assistant can understand and reply. [CONFIRM: whether calls are recorded, and the retention period.] You will be told at the start of the call.

## Safety and oversight

- The assistant only uses approved tools and approved information sources.
- It is tested against misuse, including attempts to make it ignore its instructions.
- Conversations are logged so we can review and audit its behaviour.
- Our team reviews samples regularly to check accuracy.

You can ask for a copy of your conversations or ask us to delete them, as described in our [privacy notice](/legal/privacy). Use the [data request form](/legal/data-request).
