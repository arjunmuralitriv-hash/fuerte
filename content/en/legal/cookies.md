## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. We call them all "cookies" here.

## Your choice

When you first visit, we ask for your choice. Only essential cookies run until you decide. **Reject all** is as easy as **Accept all**. You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.
