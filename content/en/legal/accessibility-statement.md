# Accessibility statement

**Last updated:** [PLACEHOLDER: date]

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people with visual, hearing, motor or cognitive disabilities. This statement applies to the Arabic and English versions of [CONFIRM: website domain].

## Our standard

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA**, in line with Qatar's digital accessibility policy and guidance from Mada (Qatar Assistive Technology Center). [CONFIRM: policy reference.]

## What we have done

- All text, including the content of the 3D story, is real HTML that screen readers can read.
- The 3D scene is decorative. A static image or short video replaces it when it cannot run.
- Motion is reduced when your device asks for reduced motion.
- Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- Colours meet AA contrast in both the light and dark themes.
- Text resizes to 200% without loss of content.
- Forms have clear labels, instructions and error messages, and errors are announced to screen readers.
- Pages work in right-to-left (Arabic) and left-to-right (English) layouts.
- Loading indicators have text labels for screen readers.
- Images that carry information have text alternatives.
