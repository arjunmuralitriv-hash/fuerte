# Personal data request

Use this form to exercise your rights under Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016). See our [privacy notice](/legal/privacy) for details.

## Form

- **What would you like to do?** (required, single choice)
  - Get a copy of my personal data
  - Correct my personal data
  - Delete my personal data
  - Withdraw my consent
  - Object to how my data is used
  - Stop marketing emails
  - Something else
- **Full name** (required)
- **Email** (required). Helper: "We'll reply to this address."
- **Phone** (optional)
- **Your relationship with Fuerte** (optional): "Client" · "Website visitor or enquirer" · "Job applicant" · "Supplier" · "Other"
- **Details** (required). Helper: "Tell us what your request is about, for example the email address you used or the date you contacted us."
- **Are you making this request for someone else?** (optional): "Yes" / "No". If yes: "We'll ask for proof that you are authorised to act for them."
- Declaration (required, unticked): "I confirm the information I've given is accurate. I understand Fuerte may need to verify my identity before acting on this request."
- **Button:** "Send request"

**Success:** "We've received your request. Your reference is {reference}. We'll confirm your identity and reply within [CONFIRM: timeframe]."

**Error (send failed):** "We couldn't send your request. Please try again, or email it to [EMAIL]."

## What happens next

1. We confirm we've received it.
2. We may ask you to verify your identity.
3. We act on your request and reply within [CONFIRM: timeframe].
4. If we cannot fully meet your request, for example because the law requires us to keep certain records, we explain why.

Making a request is free. [CONFIRM]

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the National Cyber Security Agency. [CONFIRM: competent department and contact details.]
