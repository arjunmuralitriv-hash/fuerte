# Residential compounds, towers and villas

Fuerte designs, installs and maintains compound security systems and smart technology for residential compounds, towers and private villas in Qatar. We deliver CCTV, ANPR gates, access control, video intercom, compound-wide fibre and Wi-Fi, fire alarm and smart home automation. Developers and facility managers get one contractor for handover, and residents get systems that are simple to use.

## What do compounds and residential buildings need?

- **Secure perimeters and gates** with ANPR and visitor management
- **CCTV** to MoI SSD requirements for common areas
- **Fast internet** to every villa and apartment
- **Intercom and access** that residents can use from their phones
- **Smart living** with lighting, climate and blind control
- **Reliable maintenance** for the whole community

## How do you handle CCTV and resident privacy?

Cameras cover gates, perimeters, car parks and common areas, never the inside of homes or private gardens. Signage tells residents and visitors where recording happens. Footage access is limited and logged, in line with [PDPPL](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar).
