# Banking and financial services

Fuerte helps banks, insurers and financial institutions in Qatar run secure, highly available technology. We deliver resilient branch and head-office networks, data centers, branch CCTV and access control, penetration testing and 24/7 security monitoring. Our work supports the cyber security and resilience expectations of the Qatar Central Bank (QCB) and your internal audit teams.

## What do banks need from a technology partner?

- **Uptime.** Branches, ATMs and core systems cannot go down.
- **Strict change control.** Every change planned, approved and reversible.
- **Proven security.** Regular testing, continuous monitoring and fast response.
- **Physical security.** Vaults, cash areas and data rooms watched and controlled.
- **Evidence.** Reports that satisfy auditors and regulators.

## How do we work within a bank's change control?

We follow your change management process. Every change has a method statement, risk assessment, approval, an out-of-hours window and a tested rollback plan. Our engineers follow your access and confidentiality rules.
