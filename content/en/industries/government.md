# Government

Fuerte is an IT contractor delivering secure networks, data centers, ELV systems and cyber security for ministries, agencies and government-owned entities in Qatar. We design to national requirements such as the NCSA's National Information Assurance (NIA) standard and MoI SSD rules for CCTV, and we provide the documentation public-sector procurement and audit teams expect.

## What do government entities need from a technology contractor?

- **Security by design.** Segmented networks, hardened devices and controlled access.
- **Compliance evidence.** Policies, test certificates and records that stand up to audit.
- **Continuity.** Redundant infrastructure and clear maintenance commitments.
- **Accountability.** One contractor responsible for design, delivery and support.
- **Local presence.** Engineers based in Doha who can reach your sites fast.
