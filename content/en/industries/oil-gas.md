## How do you secure operational technology (OT)?

OT systems control physical processes, so security must never disrupt them. We start with passive discovery and design reviews, separate IT and OT with controlled zones and conduits, and align with frameworks such as IEC 62443. Active testing is limited to agreed, safe scopes.
