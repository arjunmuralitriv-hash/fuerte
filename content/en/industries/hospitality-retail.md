# Hospitality and retail

Fuerte delivers IT solutions and ELV systems for hotels, serviced apartments, malls and retailers in Qatar. We install guest Wi-Fi, CCTV with people counting, access control, PA and background music, smart meeting and ballroom AV, and secure networks for payment systems. We work out of hours, so guests and shoppers never notice.

## What do hotels and malls need?

- **Great guest Wi-Fi** that is fast, simple and kept apart from business systems
- **Security and approvals** for CCTV and access, to MoI SSD requirements
- **Footfall insight** from people counting and analytics
- **Atmosphere** from background music, lighting and signage
- **Events** with ballroom and meeting-room AV
- **Payment security** with segmented networks
- **No disruption** to guests or shoppers during work

## How do you keep guest Wi-Fi separate from payment systems?

We put guests, staff, point-of-sale, CCTV and building systems on separate network segments, with firewall rules between them. A guest device can never reach a card terminal. This also supports payment card security requirements.
