# Healthcare

Fuerte delivers healthcare IT solutions, ELV systems and cyber security for hospitals, clinics, labs and pharmacies in Qatar. We build reliable networks and Wi-Fi for clinical devices, resilient server rooms, CCTV and access control for sensitive areas, and security that protects patient data. Work is planned around clinical areas and infection-control rules, so patient care is never disrupted.

## What do healthcare facilities need?

- **Always-on systems** for clinical applications and devices
- **Reliable Wi-Fi** for tablets, carts and connected equipment
- **Controlled access** to pharmacies, records and restricted wards
- **Patient privacy** for health data, which PDPPL treats as sensitive
- **Safe working** around patients, with infection-control discipline
- **Fast support** when something fails

## How do you work safely in live clinical areas?

We agree access times with your facilities team, follow infection-control procedures, use dust barriers where needed and keep noisy work away from patient areas. Every job has a method statement approved by your team.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is health data treated differently under Qatari law?
Yes. PDPPL treats health data as personal data of a special nature, with stricter rules. See our [PDPPL checklist](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar). [CONFIRM: any Ministry of Public Health requirements to reference.]

### Can you secure connected medical devices?
We segment medical devices on their own network and monitor them. We coordinate any changes with the device manufacturer.

### Do you offer 24/7 support for hospitals?
Yes, on AMC plans with 24/7 cover.
