# Education

Fuerte designs, installs and supports IT solutions for schools, colleges and universities in Qatar. We deliver high-density Wi-Fi, structured cabling, CCTV, access control, PA and bell systems, interactive classrooms and cyber security. Work is planned around the academic calendar, so upgrades happen during holidays and teaching continues without disruption.

## What do schools and campuses need?

- **Reliable Wi-Fi** in every classroom, hall and outdoor area
- **Safe campuses** with gates, CCTV and lockdown capability
- **Clear communication** with bells, paging and evacuation messages
- **Engaging classrooms** with interactive panels and easy screen sharing
- **Protected data** for students, parents and staff
- **Predictable budgets** and maintenance

## How do you protect student privacy with CCTV and data?

We position cameras to cover entrances, corridors and outdoor areas, not private spaces. Access to footage and student data is limited by role and logged. We help you set retention periods and notices in line with [PDPPL](/insights/pdppl-compliance-checklist-qatar), which gives extra protection to children's data.
